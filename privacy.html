Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains what Selene (“Selene,” “we,” “us”), an app developed by one1 Eleven dev, collects, why, and the control you have over it. Selene provides AI palm, tarot and horoscope readings for entertainment and self-reflection only. Please read it alongside our Terms of Service.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to give you a reading and keep your account working:

  • Account details. An email address (for email sign-in) or an anonymous guest identifier. Selene supports guest accounts that require no email.
  • Profile you provide. Your display name, date of birth (used to determine your zodiac sign), focus areas, chosen reader, language, and your AI and age-consent choices.
  • Content you create. Your questions, tarot draws, saved readings, chat messages with AI readers, custom spreads, and card collection.
  • Palm photos. Images you capture or upload for a palm reading — see Section 3.
  • Technical & diagnostic data. Basic app analytics and crash/diagnostic information used to keep Selene reliable and secure.

We do not ask for or intentionally collect precious sensitive identifiers such as government IDs, financial account numbers or precise location.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate your palm, tarot and horoscope readings and power chats with AI readers.
  • To create and secure your account and remember your preferences.
  • To operate, maintain, troubleshoot and improve the app.
  • To protect against abuse, fraud and security threats.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your palm photos or readings to serve advertising.

3. Palm photos

Palm photos are personal, so we treat them carefully:

  • Before upload, images are compressed and stripped of embedded metadata (such as EXIF location).
  • By default, the “keep palm photos in the cloud” setting is off, which means your photo is deleted from our servers right after your reading is generated.
  • If you turn that setting on, photos are stored securely and automatically deleted after 30 days. You can also delete all stored palm photos yourself at any time from Profile → “Delete my palm photos.”

4. AI processing

Readings are generated using Google’s Gemini models. To keep your data safe, AI requests are made only from our secure backend (Cloud Functions) — the AI provider key is never stored in the app. The relevant content of your request (for example, your question or a palm image for that reading) is sent to the model to produce your reading. We do not use your content to train third-party models.

5. Sharing & service providers

We share data only with trusted providers who process it on our behalf under contract:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database (Firestore), file storage, app-integrity checks (App Check) and analytics.
  • Google Gemini — AI generation of readings, as described above.
  • App stores & payment processors — if and when paid subscriptions are offered, purchases are handled by the relevant app store; we do not receive your full card details.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and Selene.

6. Data retention

  • Account data and saved readings are kept while your account is active.
  • Palm photos follow the rules in Section 3 (deleted immediately or within 30 days).
  • When you delete your account, your data is permanently removed — see Section 7.

7. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR and India’s DPDP Act, 2023), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent.

  • Access & edit: view and update your profile from within the app.
  • Delete photos: Profile → “Delete my palm photos.”
  • Delete everything: Profile → “Delete account & data” permanently erases your account, readings, chats and photos. Full instructions are on our Delete Account page.

You can also exercise any of these rights by emailing hello@princelabs.me. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

8. Security

We protect your data with Firebase Authentication, enforced App Check, security rules that restrict data access to your own account, and encryption in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and continually improve our safeguards.

9. Children

Selene is not directed to children. You must meet the minimum age required in your country (at least 13, or higher where local law requires) to use the app. Readings are entertainment and are not appropriate as guidance for minors. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International data transfers

Selene’s cloud infrastructure is hosted in the asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) region. If you use Selene from another country, your information may be processed in India and other countries where our providers operate, with appropriate safeguards in place.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice. Continued use of Selene after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy? Selene is developed by one1 Eleven dev. Email us at hello@princelabs.me.

✨ Reminder: Selene’s readings are for entertainment and self-reflection only, and are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.
