What’s inside

One app, many ways to reflect

Every reading is generated fresh for you and written in plain, encouraging language.

AI Palm Reading

Snap your palm and get a gentle read of your heart, head, life and fate lines, plus love, career and life-path insights.

Tarot Readings

Ask a question, pick a spread and draw from the full 78-card Rider–Waite–Smith deck with per-position meanings and advice.

Daily Horoscope

Today, week, month and year outlooks for your sign, with love, career, health and lucky highlights, plus moon phases.

AI Readers & Chat

Chat with distinct reader personas who can draw cards mid-conversation and answer follow-ups in your language.

Card Meanings

A searchable library of all 78 tarot cards with upright and reversed meanings — available offline, anytime.

Private by Design

How it works

From question to insight in seconds

1

Set your intention

Create a profile with your name and birthday, then choose what you’d like to explore — a question, a spread or your sign.

2

Let Selene read

Our AI interprets your palm, cards or chart and writes a clear, personal reading just for this moment.

3

Reflect & revisit

Save readings, continue the conversation with a reader, and return whenever you need a fresh perspective.

Built on trust

Your readings stay yours

Selene is designed to be private and transparent from the first tap.

Delete anytime

Remove your palm photos or wipe your entire account and data from within the app in a couple of taps.

Photos handled with care

Images are stripped of location metadata and, by default, deleted from our servers right after your reading.

Secure by default

Data is protected with Firebase Authentication, App Check and encrypted cloud storage.

GDPR & DPDP aligned

We honour access and deletion requests and never sell your personal data.
