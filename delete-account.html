Delete Your Account & Data

Applies to the Selene app · developer: one1 Eleven dev

You can permanently delete your Selene account and all of your data at any time. This page explains how to do it inside the app and how to request deletion if you can no longer access the app.

⚠ This cannot be undone

Deleting your account permanently erases your profile, saved readings, chats, custom spreads and palm photos. There is no way to recover them afterwards.

Option 1 · Delete in the app (recommended)

The fastest way — it removes everything instantly:

  1. Open Selene and make sure you’re signed in to the account you want to remove.
  2. Go to the Profile tab using the person icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap “Delete account & data.” You’ll find it at the bottom of the Profile screen.
  4. Confirm deletion. When the confirmation dialog appears, tap “Delete” to permanently erase your account, readings, chats and photos.

Prefer to remove only your palm images? Use Profile → “Delete my palm photos” to clear stored photos while keeping your account.

Option 2 · Request deletion by email

If you can’t access the app (for example, you’ve changed devices), email us and we’ll delete your account for you:

  • Write to hello@princelabs.me from the email address on your account.
  • Use the subject line “Delete my Selene account.”
  • We may ask a question to verify you own the account before deleting it, to protect your data.
  • We’ll confirm once your account and data have been removed, within 30 days (usually much sooner).

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile (name, date of birth, zodiac, preferences and consents).
  • All saved readings — palm, tarot and horoscope.
  • Your AI-reader chat history.
  • Custom spreads and your card collection.
  • Any palm photos stored in the cloud.
  • Your authentication record, so the account can no longer be signed in to.

What may be retained

We may retain a limited amount of information where the law requires it — for example, transaction or billing records held by the app stores for tax and accounting, or minimal logs needed for security and fraud prevention. Any retained data is kept only for as long as necessary and is not used to identify you for other purposes. See our Privacy Policy for details.

✨ Note: subscriptions purchased through the App Store or Google Play are billed by the store. Deleting your Selene account does not cancel an active subscription — manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Need help?

If you have trouble deleting your account, email hello@princelabs.me and we’ll help.
